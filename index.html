Hunger games

The Hunger Games is a dystopian book and film series by Suzanne Collins about a ruined North American
nation called Panem, where the wealthy Capitol forces poor districts to send teenagers to compete in a televised fight to the death.
Core Premise and HistoryThe Setting: Panem consists of an opulent governing city called the Capitol and 12 impoverished outlying districts
that specialize in various trades.
The Purpose: The annual Hunger Games serve as entertainment for the Capitol and function as a brutal reminder of its total power over
the districts following a failed historical rebellion.The Selection: Every year, one boy and one girl aged 12 to 18 from each district are chosen by a lottery system called the "reaping" to fight until
only one survivor remains.Main StorylineKatniss Everdeen: The original trilogy follows 16-year-old Katniss from impoverished District 12, who volunteers to take her younger sister's place in the games.
